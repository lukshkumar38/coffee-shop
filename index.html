Our Craft & Sourcing

At The Velvet Bean, we believe coffee is an experience, not just a beverage. Our journey begins at high-altitude micro-lots around the globe.

We source only the finest Arabica beans, ensuring ethical practices and fair compensation for our farmers. Each batch is meticulously roasted in-house to bring out its unique flavor profile, and our master baristas craft every cup with precision and passion. Discover a world of rich aromas, deep complexities, and unparalleled smoothness.
